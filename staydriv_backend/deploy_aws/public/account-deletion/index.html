Data Deletion & Statutory Retention Disclosure

StayDriv complies fully with Google Play's User Data policy and applicable statutory Indian digital privacy laws (Information Technology Act 2000, IT Rules 2021, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act). Below is the comprehensive disclosure of data erased versus data legally retained.

Account Deletion Process & Expected Completion Time

Stage 1: Identity & Ownership Verification (Immediate)
You submit this online request and verify account ownership via a 6-digit SMS OTP sent to your registered mobile number.
Stage 2: Instant Account Deactivation & Session Invalidation (Within 1 Minute)
All active app sessions are immediately revoked. The user cannot log in, request rides, or accept bookings. For Pilot partners, driver listings and live tracking broadcast are permanently stopped.
Stage 3: Reconciliation & Cooling Period (Days 1 to 7)
Pending trip settlements, ride dispute resolutions, or pending wallet withdrawal requests are reconciled. Users may contact support if the deletion was submitted in error.
Stage 4: Permanent Data Purge & Pseudonymization (Completed within 30 Days)
All personal identifiers, profile photos, telemetry, and documents are permanently purged from active production databases and S3 storage within 30 statutory days.

PERMANENTLY DELETED Personal Data Erased

  • Profile Information: Name, phone number, email address, profile picture, gender, and emergency contacts.
  • Authentication Credentials: Password hashes, session tokens, JWTs, and biometric app credentials.
  • Device & Notification Data: FCM push notification tokens, device hardware IDs, IP associations.
  • Saved Locations: Favorite places, home and work coordinates, custom route history.
  • Live Telemetry: Real-time GPS breadcrumbs, speed, and continuous movement tracks.
  • Pilot Documents: Driver license photos, vehicle RC copies, insurance uploads, selfie biometric photos.
  • In-App Communications: Masked call records and chat messages between rider and pilot.

STATUTORILY RETAINED Data Retained for Legal Compliance

  • Invoices & Tax Records (7 Years): Invoices, GST tax calculations, and trip billing ledgers retained under Section 44AA of the Indian Income Tax Act 1961 & CGST Act 2017.
  • Payment Gateway Logs (5 Years): Digital payment settlement IDs and transaction references retained pursuant to RBI Guidelines (PSSR Act 2007) & PMLA 2002.
  • Security & Access Logs (180 Days): Server IP access and audit logs retained pursuant to Rule 3(1)(h) of the IT Rules 2021 and CERT-In directions under Section 70B of the IT Act.
  • Active Dispute Files: Records pertaining to pending police escalations, insurance claims, or legal disputes retained until formal case closure.
